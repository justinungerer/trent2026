# Blackout Run

Open [index.html](index.html) directly in a browser, or serve this repository locally and visit /blackout-run/. Each level is available from the standalone playtest menu. In Chapter 1, the campaign opens each level in order after its story film. Quiz scores of eight, nine, and ten add one, two, or three falls between checkpoint retries. Drag to steer and hold the Brake button to slow down. Pause opens the checkpoint retry and level selection.

The main trail is paced for about three minutes per level. Steer away from the marked areas when rifle fire starts or a bomber flies over. A return trail brings Trent back to a missed pouch or signal post. The checkpoint retry keeps completed objectives.

## Painted artwork

The rifleman, bomb, and explosion reuse painted artwork from the other minigames.

The built-in ImageGen tool generated the following source paintings for this prototype. The game uses WebP copies and transparent crops from the two sprite sheets.

- **Valley.** Gouache panorama of a blue-hour Alpine valley with a moon, snow-covered firs, and an open downhill slope.
- **Forest.** Painted view down a snowy Alpine valley with a teal twilight sky, ridge lights, and tiny cabins.
- **Airstrip.** Nighttime Alpine valley with a distant 1940s airstrip, golden runway lights, and shadowed pines.
- **Snow.** Overhead texture of blue-silver powder and shallow snowdrifts.
- **Trail sprite sheet.** Three rear-view skiing poses, evergreen, boulder, courier satchel, relay cabin, and lantern sign.
- **Outpost sprite sheet.** Allied radio operator, snow-covered aircraft hangar, oil lantern, and ski-brake icon.
- **Bomber.** Painted orthographic side view of a 1940s twin-engine bomber in level flight.
- **Fall sprite sheet.** Four painted rear-view poses showing Trent stumbling, tumbling, sliding, and getting up.
- **Forest obstacles.** Painted snow-covered stumps and horizontal fallen trees in two variants.
- **Skiing sprite sheet.** Rear views of Trent gliding, tucking, carving, and planting a pole.
