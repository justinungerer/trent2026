These paintings were generated for this repository. They are original artwork for this game, not a third-party license.

## trent.webp

Horizontal strip of equal frames on a transparent background. The feet share one baseline.

- Frame width: 412 px
- Frame height: 628 px
- Frame count: 4
- Order: contact, passing, contact, passing. A walk to the right.
- Anchor: (168, 618) in every frame, at the feet. X is the center of the lower sole. Y is the shared sole baseline.
- Milliseconds per frame: 150 while Trent is moving. Standing holds the first frame. Reduced motion holds the second frame while moving.
