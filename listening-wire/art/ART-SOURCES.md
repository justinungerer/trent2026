# Listening Wire art sources

These paintings were generated for this repository. They are original artwork for this game, not a third-party license.

## trent.webp

Horizontal strip of equal frames on a transparent background. The feet share one baseline.

- Frame width: 278 px
- Frame height: 676 px
- Frame count: 5
- Order: idle, idle, raise, press, recover. Raise is the hand lifting. Press is the hand down on the key. Recover is the hand coming back.
- Anchor: (105, 666) in every frame, at the feet. X is the center of the soles. Y is the shared sole baseline.
- Milliseconds per frame:
  - idle: 520
  - raise: the first 80 ms of a send
  - press: while Send is held after that
  - recover: 160 after the key comes up

## scene.webp

Painted snowy night yard, opaque. 1280 × 720 px. Pines, a fence, a crate, and a shed with one warm window. No people and no text.

## lamp.webp

A searchlight head on a transparent background, 360 × 470 px. The game rotates it around the yoke, about 48% across and 78% down the bitmap, so the lens follows the sweep. No beam is painted into the sprite.
