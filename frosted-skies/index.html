CHAPTER 01 / NORTH POLE AIRMAIL

FROSTED SKIES

Santa’s transponder just went dark. The CIA has fitted Trent’s sleigh with a twin laser and a suspiciously warm fruitcake. Intercept the air convoy and recover Santa’s last broadcast.

Drag anywhere to steer. Every weapon you carry fires at once, spread outward from the sleigh. The nose-mounted twin laser is always equipped. Collect up to four extra weapons: flame, missiles, lasers, or heavy homing missiles. A new pickup replaces the oldest extra weapon when all mounts are full. A hull hit leaves the nose laser. On a keyboard, use WASD or the arrow keys. Dodge amber fire.
