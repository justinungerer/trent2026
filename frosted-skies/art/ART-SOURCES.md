# Art sources — Frosted Skies skiff, hauler, and wisp

These three files are original generated paintings made for this game. They are not stock images, not traced from `sleigh.webp` or `drone.webp`, and not third-party sprites.

| File | Pixels | Frames | Depicts |
|------|--------|--------|---------|
| `skiff.webp` | 447 × 512 | 1 | Small fast twin-engine craft |
| `hauler.webp` | 768 × 594 | 1 | Larger slow cargo craft |
| `wisp.webp` | 315 × 512 | 1 | Small amber dart |

Each file is a single still frame on a transparent background. The game already turns a defeated craft into a parcel; these sprites do not include a defeat animation.

No license name or external source URL applies. `sleigh.webp`, `drone.webp`, `boss.webp`, and `valley.webp` were left unchanged.
