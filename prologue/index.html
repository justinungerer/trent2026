6 June 1944

Landing at Normandy

Trent steps off the landing craft at Normandy. The wall is to the right.

Use the stick to walk. Curved waves run from right to left at different heights on the beach. A wave pushes Trent back toward the landing craft until he braces. Bracing cancels that push. Go around the wire, the hedgehogs, the craters, and the mines.

Soldiers along the beach sometimes shoot. Step clear of the gunfire and the grenades. Tap Fire to stop a soldier. Aim with the stick, including while you brace. He starts with ten bullets. Crates on the sand and packages dropped by soldiers each give two bullets.

Mines explode and cost one life. Painted hearts restore a life. Three stumbles send Trent back to the ramp.
