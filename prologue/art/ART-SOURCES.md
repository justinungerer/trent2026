# Prologue art sources

These paintings were generated for this repository. They are original artwork for this game, not a third-party license, and they are not copied from Frosted Skies or any other pack.

## trent.webp

Horizontal strip of equal frames on a transparent background. The feet share one baseline.

- Frame width: 539 px
- Frame height: 574 px
- Frame count: 14
- Order: idle (2), walk (6), brace (2), stumble (4)
- Anchor: (327, 564) in every frame, at the feet. X is the same body point under the helmet. Y is the shared sole baseline.
- Milliseconds per frame:
  - idle: 480
  - walk: 125 at a full walk (the cycle advances with distance, so a slower walk holds each frame longer)
  - brace: 180
  - stumble: 263 (the four frames divide the 1.05 second stumble)

## soldier.webp

Horizontal strip of equal frames on a transparent background. The feet share one baseline. These paintings are original generated art for this game, not a third-party license.

- Frame width: 516 px
- Frame height: 552 px
- Frame count: 10
- Order: walk (4), fire (2), hit (2), wrap (2)
- Anchor: (206, 539) in every frame, at the feet. X is the same body point under the helmet. Y is the shared sole baseline. The wrapped parcel sits on that same baseline.
- Milliseconds per frame:
  - walk: 120 while the soldier is moving. A soldier who is standing still holds the first walk frame.
  - fire: 120
  - hit: 120
  - wrap: 140 (the second frame then holds on the finished parcel)

## Shingle staging ground

`shingle-staging.webp` was generated with the built-in image generation tool for prologue level 2 on November 18, 2026. It shows the inland shingle ground between the landing beach and the breached defenses.

The generation prompt was “High resolution painted semi-realistic WWII game environment, landscape 1536x1024. A shingle staging ground just inland of Normandy beach between the sand landing and the breached coastal defenses. Near top 24 percent distant grey cloudy sky, earth berms and concrete fortifications at horizon. Lower 76 percent expansive nearly top-down gravel shingle and flattened grass paths, scattered subtle rubble and footprints, enough clear walking space to move vertically and horizontally. Cool subdued realistic oil painted game-art texture, readable ground plane, no people, no text, no pickup objects, no vehicles, no isolated foreground obstacles. Context is transition from the beach seawall to inland shelter.”

## Painted surf

Built-in image generation produced three transparent painted surf variants. The game bends each texture to fit the original wave crest.


### painted-surf-1.webp

> High-resolution painted 2D game sprite of cold ocean surf rolling left across a Normandy beach, seen from directly overhead. Genuine transparent alpha background. A single continuous long, narrow vertical strip of white sea foam and translucent cold gray-blue water. Keep the leading breaking crest nearly straight, from the top of the sprite almost to the bottom, aligned at about 25 percent of the image width from the left edge. The thin bright crest faces left. Water and trailing foam extend to its right and dissolve softly into transparency. The strip fills the image height, fades at both tips, and has transparent gutters at all edges. Game asset suitable for stretching into a curved breaker at runtime. Semi-realistic painted brushwork, clearly visible white foam, distinct brush textures. Only an isolated wave crest and shallow trailing wash. No sand, horizon, beach background, coastline, objects, text, outlines, or shadow. Portrait canvas. A narrow wind-swept white crest with feathery gray-blue spray and delicate lace foam.
