NORTH POLE FIELD DISPATCH

Special delivery

Sprout hid three dispatches in the freight yard. Find them and reach the mail hatch. The clockwork inspectors are under strict orders to confiscate anything with a bow.

Left thumb on the pad to walk. Right thumb for a decoy. WASD or arrows, and Space, still work. Yellow discs are mines. Sweeping lights call the inspectors.
