# Night Broadcast art sources

These paintings were generated for this repository. They are original artwork for this game, not a third-party license.

## trent.webp

Horizontal strip of equal frames on a transparent background. The feet share one baseline.

- Frame width: 235 px
- Frame height: 513 px
- Frame count: 6
- Order: idle (2), tuning (4). The tuning frames keep both hands on a small brass dial.
- Anchor: (80, 497) in every frame, at the feet. X is the center of the soles. Y is the shared sole baseline.
- Milliseconds per frame:
  - idle: 480
  - tuning: 160 while the dial is being dragged or an arrow is held

## scene.webp

Painted snowy night outside a small shelter, opaque. 1600 × 900 px. A warm lamp by the entrance. No text.

## radio.webp

Transparent background. Brass field radio, 947 × 674 px. The dial face and the signal meter are blank so the canvas needle and meter can sit on them.
