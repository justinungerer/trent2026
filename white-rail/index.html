Reach the end of the 1,560 m line to win. Any track will do.

Use Left and Right or swipe sideways to pick a track. Tap Pump with your right thumb for each stroke. Stop tapping to coast, or hold Brake above Pump to slow down twice as fast. Brake takes priority while held.

Every plane bombs the rail you are on when it drops its bomb. Switch tracks when you see the orange impact marker. Slow down when you need time to pick a safe track.

You start with a three-hit limit. Each quiz bonus adds one extra hit to your limit. Crates and lowered signal arms count as one hit each. Rail gaps count as two. After a hit, you are protected for 3 seconds.

Distant tanks fire at your current track. Change tracks after a tank fires to dodge its shell. A shell counts as one hit.
