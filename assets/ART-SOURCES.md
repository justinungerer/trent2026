## Painted chapter-menu Christmas-light dividers

`chapter-menu-lights.webp` was generated with the built-in image generation tool. The transparent source was inspected and cropped to the visible strand with a small margin, then converted to WebP preserving alpha. It decorates the boundaries between chapter groups.

Prompt:

A single very wide horizontal strand of Christmas lights as a hand painted divider graphic for a game menu. Panoramic banner about 1536 by 192 pixels. Thin dark green cable gently draped in shallow scallops, evenly spaced small glowing painted red, emerald green, and warm golden Christmas bulbs, a few tiny snowy highlights. Detailed realistic painted texture, tasteful festive WWII Christmas adventure game art. One continuous strand, all bulbs fully visible. No text, no border, no extra decorations. Truly transparent alpha-zero background, no rectangular backdrop or haze. Fill the wide frame with minimal empty margins above and below.

## Limitations

- Commons rights statements are the basis for reuse of the Commons copies. The IWM website separately presents its downloadable copy of E 1772 under non-commercial terms; it was used for identification, not as the source of the local download. Check jurisdiction and intended distribution before publication, especially U.S.-government public-domain status outside the U.S.
- Tank-04 and tank-05 originate from low-resolution photographs and their 960-pixel renditions are upscaled; no extra detail was created. Tank-05 is partly obscured by vegetation. Tank-07's M3/M5 subtype is not established by its Commons caption.
- Museum photos depict preserved examples, not necessarily wartime photographs. Some images include background vehicles; the question identifies the foreground subject where relevant. No vehicle-name plaque is readable in the delivered images after the two crops.
